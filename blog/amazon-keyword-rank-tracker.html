Track your ASIN's Amazon keyword rank on 20 marketplaces

By Divya Raj · Updated 9 November 2026 · 5 min read

Quick answer

Amazon Keyword Rank Tracker checks where your ASINs rank for each keyword on about 20 Amazon marketplaces. Each row gives the organic rank, the position shoppers see, the page, whether it was an ad, and the product's name, price, rating, reviews and monthly sales estimate, plus how the rank changed since the last run. It costs $0.01 per ASIN found per keyword, with no subscription.

Why choose Amazon Keyword Rank Tracker?

  1. Pay per ASIN, not per pageYou pay $0.01 for each tracked ASIN it finds, not for every product it scans on the way. ASINs it doesn't find are reported and not charged.
  2. Rank change built inIt remembers the last run and reports whether each rank went up or down, and by how much.
  3. Organic and ad positions apartOrganic rank, shopper-visible position, page and ad type are separate fields, so ads don't distort your SEO rank.
  4. Product context in every rowName, price, list price, rating, reviews, badges and estimated monthly sales sit next to each rank.
  5. More marketplacesAbout 20 Amazon marketplaces, more than the keyword tools below, and local results by delivery postal code.
  6. No subscriptionRun it when you need it, or schedule it daily for a rank history.

How many marketplaces do Amazon keyword tools on Apify cover?

Marketplace counts are from each tool's Apify Store description: memo23 keyword research, khadinakbar product search and doesaiknow keyword research.

Frequently asked questions

How much does it cost to track 10 keywords for 3 ASINs every day?

About $9.15 a month: 900 rank rows at $0.01 plus 30 runs at $0.005, if every ASIN is found every day.

What happens if my ASIN is not found?

It is reported as not found within the pages scanned, and you are not charged a rank row for it.

Does it show sponsored positions?

Yes. Organic rank and ad position are separate fields, with the ad type.

Can I track competitors' ASINs?

Yes. Add any ASIN, yours or a competitor's.

Can I check rank for a specific city or region?

Yes. Set a delivery ZIP or postal code to see local results.
