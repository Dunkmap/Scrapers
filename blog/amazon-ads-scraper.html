See every ad on an Amazon search page, with no per-result fee

By Divya Raj · Updated 9 November 2026 · 4 min read

Quick answer

Amazon Ads Scraper collects the ads on Amazon search pages: brand banners with their headline and featured ASINs, sponsored video ads and sponsored products, each with keyword, page, placement, price and rating. It works on about 20 Amazon marketplaces and has no per-result fee: you pay only for the Apify platform usage of each run.

Why choose Amazon Ads Scraper?

  1. Built only for adsEvery row is an ad, so you don't pay for, or filter out, organic listings.
  2. All three ad formatsBrand banners (headline, brand and featured ASINs), sponsored video ads and sponsored products in one dataset.
  3. No per-result feeSearch scrapers that flag sponsored listings charge $1 to $2 per 1,000 rows. This actor charges nothing per row; only Apify platform usage applies.
  4. Placement detailEach ad records its placement type, such as top-of-page brand banner, sponsored video row or inline sponsored product.
  5. 20 marketplacesCompare who advertises on the same keyword in the US, UK, Germany, India, Japan and more.

What do other scrapers charge to collect Amazon ads?

No other dedicated Amazon ads scraper turned up in the Apify Store. The closest alternatives are Amazon search scrapers that flag sponsored listings: apt_marble, santamaria-automations and khadinakbar.

What you get in every row

One row per ad:

  • Searchkeyword, marketplace, page
  • Ad typebrand banner, video ad or sponsored product
  • ProductsASIN or featured ASINs, brand storefront
  • Creativeheadline, title or video label
  • Offerdisplayed price, rating, review count

Frequently asked questions

Is Amazon Ads Scraper free?

It has no per-result fee. You pay only Apify platform usage for each run, which the Apify free plan's monthly credit covers for light use.

Which ad types does it collect?

Brand banners with their featured ASINs, sponsored video ads and sponsored products.

Can I track competitors' ads over time?

Yes. Schedule it daily or weekly on the same keywords and compare the datasets.

Does it need an Amazon Ads account?

No. It reads public search pages and needs no login.
