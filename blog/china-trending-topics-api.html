Quick answer

China Trending Topics API collects live trending topics from Baidu (百度热搜), Douyin (抖音热搜), Zhihu, Bilibili, Toutiao, Tieba and The Paper into one dataset, with rank and heat changes between runs and keyword alerts to Slack, Telegram or Discord. It costs $2 per 1,000 topics, a quarter of the price of dedicated Douyin hot-search scrapers on Apify.

Frequently asked questions

What is 今日热榜?

It means "today's hot lists": the trending boards Chinese platforms publish. This actor collects seven of them in one run.

Does it include Douyin and Baidu hot search?

Yes, plus Zhihu, Bilibili, Toutiao, Tieba and The Paper.

How much does it cost?

$2 per 1,000 topics. Hourly runs that return only new topics keep the cost low.

Do I need a Chinese account?

No. It needs no login.

Can I get alerts for my brand?

Yes. Add keywords and a Slack, Telegram or Discord destination.
