Why choose LinkedIn Jobs Scraper + Glassdoor?

  1. Two job sites, one datasetLinkedIn and Glassdoor are searched in the same run and returned in one schema, with duplicates removed across both sites. None of the four most-used LinkedIn job scrapers on Apify search Glassdoor.
  2. Full details at the base priceThe full description, applicant count, seniority, skills, salary, company details and recruiter contacts cost the same $0.40 per 1,000 jobs. Some popular alternatives charge extra for company data.
  3. Fresh jobs, one per companyFilter to jobs posted in the last hour, keep one job per company, and turn on "only new jobs" for scheduled runs: jobs you already collected are skipped, so you only pay for fresh listings.
  4. Glassdoor ratings next to LinkedIn jobsEach job can carry the employer's Glassdoor rating, so you can filter out low-rated companies before you apply or pitch.
  5. AI search and match scoresDescribe the job you want in plain English, or score each job against your profile, using your own Groq key.
  6. Fast and login-freeIt runs over plain HTTP with no browser and no account. About 850 LinkedIn jobs come back in 87 seconds.

What you get in every row

Each job is one row in a JSON, CSV or Excel export. With details turned on, a row holds:

  • Jobtitle, job type, remote / hybrid / on-site, posted time
  • Paysalary range and its source
  • Skillsskills pulled from the posting
  • Companyname, size, industry, website, address
  • RatingsGlassdoor company rating
  • Peoplerecruiter contact when shown, applicant count and early-applicant flag

Frequently asked questions

How much does it cost to scrape 10,000 LinkedIn jobs?

On the Apify free plan it costs $4.00 for 10,000 jobs ($0.40 per 1,000), with full details included. Higher Apify plans pay less, down to $0.30 per 1,000 jobs.

Do I need a LinkedIn account or cookies?

No. The scraper reads public job listings over plain HTTP, with no login, no cookies and no browser.

Does it remove duplicates between LinkedIn and Glassdoor?

Yes. When the same job appears on both sites, it is kept once, so your dataset has one row per job.

Can it collect only new jobs every day?

Yes. Turn on "only new jobs" and schedule a daily run. Jobs collected in earlier runs are skipped and not charged.

Which formats can I export?

JSON, CSV and Excel from the Apify console, or the same data through the Apify API, n8n, Make or Zapier.

Can AI agents use it?

Yes. It works in ChatGPT, Claude and n8n, and as an MCP server, so AI agents can run job searches and get structured results back.
