Frequently asked questions

Which Amazon marketplaces does it support?

About 20, including amazon.com, amazon.co.uk, amazon.de, amazon.fr, amazon.it, amazon.es, amazon.ca, amazon.in, amazon.co.jp and amazon.com.au.

Does it separate sponsored and organic results?

Yes. Each row says whether it is sponsored, and organic results get their own organic-only rank.

How are monthly sales estimated?

From the "bought in past month" label Amazon shows on many listings.

Do I pay for failed pages?

No. Error rows are written for diagnosis but never charged. Besides results, the only fee is $0.001 per run start (per GB of memory).

Do I need an Amazon account?

No. It needs no login. Residential proxies are recommended for the most reliable results.
