What you get

Every job field you need, in one spreadsheet

Jobs Exporter collects more than the job list. Turn on the details you want and each one becomes a column in your export.

Job list

Title, company, location, posted date and job link for every job in your search.

Auto-scroll & scrape

Goes through up to 10 result pages per run for you, so you don't have to click through them.

Job details

Salary, experience level, workplace type, job type, applicant count, full description and apply link.

Skills match

The skills each job asks for, and which of them match your own profile.

Company details

Industry, size, headquarters, founded year, specialties and website for each employer.

Hiring team

The job poster's name, title and profile link, saved in a separate "Hiring Team" sheet.

Optional AI clean-up. Jobs Exporter can fill in missing salary, experience and workplace details from the job description and remove off-topic skills. It uses Chrome's built-in on-device AI, so it's free and nothing leaves your computer.

How it works

  1. 1Install Jobs Exporter and sign in with Google.
  2. 2Open a LinkedIn or Glassdoor job search.
  3. 3Click Auto-scroll & scrape.
  4. 4Export to Excel (XLSX), CSV or JSON.

FAQ

Questions about Jobs Exporter

Is Jobs Exporter free?

You get one free trial run with every Premium field, up to 20 jobs. Premium costs $10 a month or $100 a year for up to 3,100 jobs a month, or you can buy a 1-week pass for $6 with no subscription.

Can I export LinkedIn jobs to Excel?

Yes. Open a LinkedIn job search, click Auto-scroll & scrape, and export the jobs to Excel (XLSX), CSV or JSON.

Does it work on Glassdoor?

Yes. It exports jobs from Glassdoor job searches the same way it does from LinkedIn.

Where is my data stored?

The jobs you collect are stored only in your browser and are never sent to my servers. Google sign-in is used only to link your free trial and Premium plan to your account.

How many jobs can I export in one run?

Each run scrolls through up to 10 result pages. For 10,000 or more jobs per run, use the LinkedIn Jobs Scraper + Glassdoor on Apify, which runs in the cloud.

Is Jobs Exporter made by LinkedIn or Glassdoor?

No. Jobs Exporter is an independent extension and is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by LinkedIn or Glassdoor.
